## About the Movie
**The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies** is a fantasy adventure movie released in *2014*. It is the last movie of The Hobbit trilogy and was directed by Peter Jackson.

The movie is about Bilbo Baggins and the dwarves after they finally reached the Lonely Mountain. However, things started to get complicated because different groups wanted to claim the mountain and its treasure. This eventually led to a huge battle where elves, dwarves, humans, and orcs were involved.

## Main Characters
Some of the main characters in the movie are:
1. **Bilbo Baggins** - The hobbit who joined the adventure.
2. **Thorin Oakenshield** - The leader of the dwarves.
3. **Gandalf** - The wizard who helped them.
4. **Legolas** - An elf who is really good with a bow.
5. **Bard** - The man who defeated Smaug.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because <mark>I enjoy watching fantasy and adventure movies</mark>. The battle scenes were really good, and I also like how the story connects to *The Lord of the Rings*.

Some of the things I liked about the movie are:
- **The battle scenes**, especially <mark>Legolas' battle scene</mark> where he was hanging onto the legs of a giant war bat while fighting the orcs.
- **The characters**, especially <mark>Thranduil</mark> (Legolas' father) because he looked so calm and powerful.
- **The story**. I liked the part where Bilbo secretly gave the Arkenstone to Bard and Thranduil because he wanted to prevent a war. It showed that <mark>he cared more about his friends than the treasure</mark>.
- **The places shown in the movie**. I liked the Lonely Mountain and Dale, especially <mark>how the mountain looked during the battle</mark>. I also liked the scenes in Ravenhill where Thorin and the others fought the orcs.
